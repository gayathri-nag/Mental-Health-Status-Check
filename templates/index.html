About This Project

Mental Health Status Check is an educational project designed to help users reflect on different aspects of their emotional well-being.

How It Works

Answer six questions related to anxiety, stress, anger, sleep, mood changes and low mood.

Quick Reminder

Take your time. Select the option that best represents your experience. There are no right or wrong answers.

✨ Your well-being matters. Make time for yourself.
